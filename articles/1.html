Some research papers which are a delight to read.

April 2026

I started reading research papers for fun and some of them were really enjoyable reads. Here are a few of my favourites.

  • SoK: Watermarking for AI-Generated Content

    This paper is a SoK (systematization of knowledge) paper that provides a comprehensive overview of the field of watermarking for AI-generated content.

  • Wavelets - Something for analytical chemistry?

    This paper explores the application of wavelet transforms in analytical chemistry, providing insights into their utility and potential limitations.

  • Automata in SageMath—Combinatorics meets Theoretical Computer Science

    I really loved how the whole paper has a plot and the story telling is engaging. The paper explores the implementation of automata in SageMath and its applications in combinatorial mathematics and theoretical computer science.
