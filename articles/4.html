Things Mr. William Shakespeare said (hopefully)

April 2026

Some things that Mr Shakespeare said (or wrote) that have stayed with me ever since I read them.

  • Before you speak, listen
    Before you write, think
    Before you spend, earn
    Before you pray, forgive
    Before you hurt, feel
    Before you hate, love
    Before you quit, try
    Before you die, LIVE
  • O God, O God, how weary, stale, flat and unprofitable seem to me all the uses of this world!
  • All the world's a stage, and all the men and women merely players. They have their exits and their entrances; and a man in his time plays many parts.
