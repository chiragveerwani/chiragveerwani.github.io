FOSSEE Summer Fellowship 2026

May 2026 - July 2026

I was selected for the FOSSEE Summer Fellowship, a highly competitive national initiative funded by the National Mission on Education through ICT (NMEICT), Ministry of Education, Government of India, and managed by IIT Bombay.

FOSSEE (Free and Open-Source Software for Education) promotes the adoption and use of open-source software tools in academia and research to reduce dependency on proprietary software.

During the fellowship, I worked on three case studies that involved applying open-source software tools to solve real-world problems by implementing research papers.

Case Studies:

1. Case Study 1: Modeling and Simulation of Satellite Orbit Dynamics and Orbit Controller Using Scilab and Xcos.

2. Case Study 2: Interactive GUI-Based Implementation of Gentamicin Elution Kinetics from Antibiotic-Loaded PMMA Bone Cement using Scilab
