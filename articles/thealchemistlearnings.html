My Learnings from The Alchemist by Paulo Coelho.

May 2026

It is currently 12:35 am and I am reading The alchemist for the second time just to remind myself of the blessing of being able to read this masterpiece. Some of my learnings:

  • If you pray for flowers, Don't wonder why it rains.
  • In the end, everything will be okay and if it's not okay, it's not the end.
  • If you're going through hell, keep going. Why would you stop in hell?
  • The sun is alone everyday and still shines.
  • If you're irritated by every rub, how will you be polished?
  • Crying doesn't mean you're weak. Since birth, it has always been a sign that you're alive.
  • If you continue to carry the brick from your past, you will end up building the same house.
  • Sunsets are proof that there is beauty in endings.
  • Stopping your worst habit would changes your life much faster than starting your best habit.
    Fix the leak, Before filling the bucket.
  • How can you be ahead or behind in life when you are moving on a path that only you can take?
  • The 'right time' doesn't exist. There is just 'time' and what you do with it.
  • There is no happiness to the path. Happiness is the path.
